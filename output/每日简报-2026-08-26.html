CRISPR In Vivo Therapies Show Promise in Early Trials

A landmark study published in Nature demonstrates that in vivo CRISPR-Cas9 therapy reduced hereditary angioedema attacks by 95% after a single dose. Meanwhile, a phase I trial for Duchenne muscular dystrophy shows a 70% reduction in off-target effects compared to earlier generations, marking a critical step toward clinical translation.

量子计算里程碑：微软 Majorana 1 实现 100 逻辑量子比特，纠错能力跃升 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 冲击：High

微软宣布基于拓扑量子比特的 Majorana 1 芯片成功实现 100个逻辑量子比特，错误率低于0.1%，较 2025 年提升40倍。IBM 同期发布了 433 比特的 Condor 处理器，但纠错能力仍落后。这标志着量子计算从“物理比特堆砌”进入“逻辑比特实用化”阶段。

Microsoft Achieves 100 Logical Qubits, Quantum Error Correction Leaps

Microsoft's Majorana 1 chip has achieved 100 logical qubits with an error rate below 0.1%, a 40x improvement over 2025. IBM's 433-qubit Condor processor trails in error correction. This milestone marks the transition from physical qubit accumulation to practical logical qubits, a critical step for fault-tolerant quantum computing.

📖 Key Vocabulary

📖 深度解读

📈 前景：短期(1-3年)将用于量子化学模拟、组合优化；中长期(5-10年)有望破解RSA加密，推动新药研发。预计2029年实现千逻辑比特。

🔮 畅想：量子计算将彻底改变材料设计、物流调度和密码学。但后量子加密（PQC）迁移也迫在眉睫，否则现有加密体系将崩溃。

Phase-Change Memory Breakthrough Approaches DRAM Speed

Intel and TSMC's co-developed vertical Phase-Change Memory (PCM) has achieved 2ns write latency, approaching DRAM's 1.5ns, with 4x higher storage density. The technology is slated for mass production in 2027, potentially replacing some DRAM and NAND in AI servers, reducing power consumption by approximately 30%.

📈 前景：短期将用于AI加速卡上的缓存；中长期有望改变计算机体系结构，实现“存储与计算一体”。
