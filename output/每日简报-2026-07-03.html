CRISPR Gene Therapy Milestone: Casgevy In Vivo Editing

In 2026, CRISPR-based in vivo gene editing has reached a pivotal clinical milestone. Casgevy demonstrated a 94% complete remission rate in sickle cell disease and beta-thalassemia patients, marking the first highly efficient targeted gene correction in humans. Long-term safety monitoring remains essential.
