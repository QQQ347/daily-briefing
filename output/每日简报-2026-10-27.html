Nature Study Links WEE1 Kinase to Quiescent Cell Cycle Re-entry

Experimental and modelling data published in Nature indicate that WEE1 kinase promotes the re-entry of quiescent cells into the cell cycle. The finding could reshape therapeutic strategies targeting dormant tumor cells and tissue regeneration.

📖 Key Vocabulary

📖 深度解读

📈 前景：短期（1-3年）可用于联合化疗清除残留癌细胞；中长期（3-10年）或延伸至器官再生与抗衰老领域，落地需更多临床前验证。

🔮 畅想：若完全实现，癌症复发率或显著下降，"唤醒疗法"成为精准医学新分支，但也带来对细胞失控增殖的伦理与安全担忧。

Redefining the Gene: From Protein-Coding to Transcribed Sequences

NHGRI and leading textbooks now define a gene as any DNA sequence that is transcribed, including non-coding genes. The shift reflects post-ENCODE understanding of genomic complexity and affects how gene-editing targets are selected.
