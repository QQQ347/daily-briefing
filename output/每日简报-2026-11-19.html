Islands Host 20% of Global Bee Diversity, Nature Study Finds

A global analysis of bee species across 305 islands reveals that islands account for 20% of global bee diversity, with more than 4,100 species recorded. The findings underscore the vulnerability of island endemics and argue for targeted pollinator conservation strategies.

📈 前景：短期（1-3年）可为岛屿国家制定农药限制与入侵物种防控清单；中长期（3-10年）可支撑全球传粉者迁徙廊道规划与农业授粉保险定价。

Redefining the Gene: From Protein-Coding to Transcribed DNA

Contemporary definitions describe a gene as any DNA sequence that is transcribed, explicitly including non-protein-coding genes. This shift reflects a deeper understanding of non-coding RNA regulatory networks and carries implications for gene-editing target selection.

📖 Key Vocabulary

📖 深度解读

📈 前景：短期可优化 CRISPR 脱靶评估中的注释库；中长期将推动以非编码区为靶点的罕见病疗法与调控型核酸药物开发。

🔮 畅想：当"基因"边界被彻底打开，医学将从"修补蛋白"走向"调音基因组"，疾病干预维度大幅扩展，同时也带来伦理上"何为可编辑"的新争论。
