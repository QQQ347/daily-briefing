⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 冲击：High 🔴 重大风险

Huawei Announces "Tao's Law" for EUV-Free Advanced Chips; TSMC Says It's Not Revolutionary

Huawei's HiSilicon chair He Tingbo unveiled "Tao's Law" at ISCAS 2026, claiming equivalent 1.4nm performance without EUV lithography through 3D stacking and advanced packaging. TSMC's senior VP Zhang Xiaoqiang countered that the concept relies on known techniques and is not a breakthrough, noting that power and thermal limits are now the primary constraints.

📖 Key Vocabulary

📖 深度解读

📈 前景：短期（1-2年）可能在中低端芯片领域见效；中长期若散热与功耗问题无法解决，等效制程提升将遇到天花板。

🔮 畅想：如果“韬定律”被验证，半导体行业将分裂为“EUV路线”和“先进封装路线”两大阵营，重塑全球芯片供应链。
