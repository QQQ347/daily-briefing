CMG Helicase Directly Recruits PRIMPOL for Leading-Strand Synthesis

Structural and biochemical studies published in Nature demonstrate that the CMG helicase directly recruits PRIMPOL to facilitate leading-strand DNA synthesis. This mechanism sheds light on replication fork restart under genotoxic stress and may inform future strategies to overcome chemoresistance.

📖 Key Vocabulary
helicase 解旋酶 leading strand 前导链 replication fork 复制叉 genotoxic stress 基因毒性应激
📖 深度解读

📌 是什么：DNA 复制像一条高速公路，解旋酶是"开路车"，PRIMPOL 是"应急修补队"。过去认为修补队自行到场，新研究证明开路车会主动"打电话叫"修补队，这改变了复制应激响应的模型。

📈 前景：短期（1-3年）用于解释部分肿瘤对铂类药物耐药的原因；中期（3-10年）可能催生针对 PRIMPOL 通路的增敏剂，与现有化疗联用。

🔮 畅想：若该通路被精准调控，未来化疗可实现"只杀癌细胞、不伤正常增殖细胞"，显著降低脱发、骨髓抑制等副作用。

NIH and Genome.gov Refresh the Authoritative Definition of a Gene

The NIH Genetics Glossary and Genome.gov have updated their definition of a gene to include transcribed sequences that do not encode proteins. The revision aligns terminology with the growing recognition of non-coding RNAs as functional regulatory elements.

📖 深度解读
