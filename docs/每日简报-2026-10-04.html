Type 2 Inflammation Trains Macrophages to Confer Disease Tolerance Against Lethal Influenza

A study published in Nature reveals that type 2 inflammation reprograms nerve- and airway-associated macrophages, enabling disease tolerance rather than direct pathogen clearance during lethal influenza infection. This shifts antiviral strategy from eradication toward host resilience, offering a novel therapeutic axis for severe respiratory viral disease.

📖 Key Vocabulary

📖 深度解读
